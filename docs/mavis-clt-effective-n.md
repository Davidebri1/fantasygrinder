# Has the central limit theorem actually been reached?

Generated by scripts/mavis-clt-effective-n.mjs on 2026-11-07.

The accuracy harness gates on **30 earlier games**. That guarantees 30 observations of the
variable the model actually projects - DraftKings points per game - so the CLT holds for the
projection. It does **not** automatically hold for a secondary statistic: a receiver who was
targeted zero times in 20 of his 30 games has an effective sample of 10 catches, and no
number of games fixes that. This page measures the difference.

## What this means

- The CLT is reached **for the variable the model projects** (DraftKings points) at every
  qualified target game: n >= 30 earlier games.
- It is reached **per statistic** only where the share in the table above is high. Where it
  is not, that statistic's mean for that player rests on fewer than 30 real observations.
- 1420 of 5343 target games (26.6%) have at least one statistic whose effective sample
  is below 30.
